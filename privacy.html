Privacy policy

My MP Parliament · Last updated 22 October 2026

No account · No tracking

My MP Parliament (“the app”) is an independent reader for the UK Parliament’s public record. It is not made by, or affiliated with, the House of Commons or any government body. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app on iOS.

Who we are

The app is published by Ross Cameron (United Kingdom), developer of My MP Parliament. For privacy enquiries, contact support@mympparliament.uk

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, or Apple ID inside the app, and the app does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

We do not operate servers that store a personal profile about you.

Your postcode

If you enter a postcode during setup, it is sent once to a public UK postcode lookup service (postcodes.io) to determine your parliamentary constituency. We do not store your postcode on our servers. The app keeps your chosen constituency and MP on your device so it can show relevant content.

Location

If you choose to use location to find your constituency, iOS provides a one-time location fix. It is used only for that lookup and is not sent to our servers. You can use a postcode instead.

What stays on your phone

The following are stored locally on your device (for example in iOS UserDefaults):

  • Your constituency and MP details
  • MPs you follow or save
  • Topics and display preferences
  • Onboarding and app settings

You can erase this data at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy policy → Erase local data, or by deleting the app.

How we use your data

Everything above is used only on your device to:

  • Show your MP and constituency
  • Filter and highlight parliamentary activity you care about
  • Remember your preferences between launches

We do not sell or share this information with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-party services (public APIs)

The app loads public parliamentary and related data directly from UK government and Parliament services. When you use those features, your device may contact:

  • UK Parliament Members API (members-api.parliament.uk)
  • UK Parliament Hansard API (hansard-api.parliament.uk)
  • UK Parliament Commons Divisions API (commonsvotes-api.parliament.uk)
  • UK Government Postcode Lookup (postcodes.io)
  • Independent Parliamentary Standards Authority (IPSA) open data for MP expenses

Those organisations operate their own services and privacy practices. We do not control them. Requests typically include only what is needed to fetch public records (for example an MP identifier or constituency), not your name or account.

Crash reports

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers through Apple, Apple may provide anonymised crash and performance data. We do not receive your name or contact details through that channel.

Security

Local data uses iOS’s standard storage APIs. Communication with public APIs uses HTTPS. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but we minimise what we hold and do not maintain central copies of your personal preferences.

Children

The app is intended to make parliamentary information accessible to a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not require registration.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

If you are in the UK or EEA, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, the information you control is on your device—and you can delete it as described above. You may still contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
