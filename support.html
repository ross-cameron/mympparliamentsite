My MP Parliament for iPhone and iPad

My MP Parliament helps you follow your MP and explore the UK Commons public record—votes, debates, questions, expenses, and more. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with Parliament or any political party.

Common questions

How do I change my constituency or MP?

Open Settings (tab bar) → Constituency and enter a new postcode, or use location when offered. Your choice is saved on this device only.

How do I reset the app or clear my data?

Go to Settings → Privacy policy → Erase local data, or delete the app from your home screen. That removes saved MPs, topics, and preferences on this device.

Why is some data missing or out of date?

Parliamentary data comes from official public APIs and open datasets. They can lag behind the live record or occasionally be unavailable. Try again later or pull to refresh where the screen supports it.

Where does the information come from?

Sources include UK Parliament APIs, the Register of Members’ Financial Interests, IPSA expenses data, and postcode lookup for constituencies. Topic groupings and plain-English labels in the app are our own interpretation, not official Parliament classifications.

Do you have access to my account or password?

No. The app does not use accounts or sign-in. We do not run tracking or advertising in the app. See our privacy policy for details.

What is Parliament Pro?

Parliament Pro is an optional, one-time in-app purchase that unlocks every MP on the map, full search of an MP's record, MP comparisons, and the alignment quiz. It's a single payment on your Apple ID — not a subscription, and it doesn't renew or bill you again.

How do I restore my Parliament Pro purchase?

If you've reinstalled the app or are on a new device signed into the same Apple ID, open Settings (tab bar) → Parliament Pro, or open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. It's free and uses Apple's records, not an account with us.
